# Problem Statement
We propose to develop software for keeping you notified on your Steam wishlist. Many people have accumulated large wishlists on Steam, and the majority of those people just sit on their wishlists without actually playing the games they are interested in.

When a game on your wishlist goes on sale, Steam already has an option to notify you. However, Steam does not inform you whether that sale is actually a good deal compared to previous discounts.

What we aim to accomplish with our software, is to have an application that will both notify you of a sale and also cross reference that deal with the game's timeline of sales, to both inform you of the current discount, if that discount is a good deal for that game based on its previous sales and also how
often the game may be going on sale.
# Stakeholder Analysis
The application will be of use to people who play games on PC or through the Steam marketplace. 

The main appeal is for people who have large wishlists, but can be useful to anyone who wishlists games on Steam.
# Key Features
The application in question will contain these features:

● View and manage your Steam wishlist

● Notifications on Steam sales

● Timelines of sales

● Whether or not it is a good time to purchase based on price history

● Sale frequency tracking to show how often games go on sale.
